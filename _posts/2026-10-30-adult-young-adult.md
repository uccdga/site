**Mark’s Pastoral Reflection -** _God’s mysterious movements in life and community._

The Saturday before our Sunday school ministry started for the year, a group of us gathered to pray. We started in the sanctuary, huddled together in the front pews for several minutes. During our prayer time, a man entered the sanctuary, sat in a pew towards the back, and prayed privately. Afterwards, I had the opportunity to introduce myself and to learn his story. Fast forwarding a bit, he and I now meet for coffee on a regular basis, settling on a monthly cadence the first week every month. Our central and repeated topic of conversation is the importance of human connection and the desire to know God. Isn’t God mysterious in His ways? _Pray for this man and for the Spirit’s guidance as we faithfully connect with others in our community._

**25-35-Somethings Small Group** - _An important group in our community that continues to grow…_

This growing group continues to meet weekly on **Wednesdays, 4:30pm-5:30pm**. The last several weeks, we have been taking turns bringing a topic of conversation to the group. Most recently it has included Christian nationalism (these people aren’t afraid of the big topics!), Sabbath-keeping, and helping people while maintaining boundaries. We are now considering a second small group for 18-35 during the week, as well!
