As part of our desire to provide meaningful ways to care for and connect with our church family, we are now offering  **Prayer Support**  following each Sunday worship service.

Sometimes we carry burdens that are difficult to share publicly, yet we long for someone to pray with us. Prayer Support offers a quiet, caring opportunity to receive personal prayer in a private setting.

Our Prayer Support Team is made up of trained Care Ministers and Stewards who are prepared to listen with compassion and pray with you. Following the service, they will be available in the Narthex and will greet you if you would like someone to pray with or for you. Whether you are facing a challenge, celebrating an answered prayer, seeking guidance, or simply desiring encouragement, they would be honored to spend a few moments in prayer with you.

You don't have to carry life's joys and burdens alone. We invite you to stop by after worship whenever you would like someone to pray alongside you...
