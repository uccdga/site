**Pastoral Reflection -** _An encouraging interaction with the next generation…_

Mid-September, I was reading a theology book for school at a local coffee shop. A man in his 20’s sat next to me, noticed what I was reading, and asked me about it (it was a book on scriptural interpretation). This began a 30-minute conversation about Jesus and the faith, something which this young man is interested in learning more about. Like many other young men in his demographic, he is interested in going to church, consumes faith-based content online, and has an affinity for higher-church traditions. When he found out our church has an organ, he asked if our organist has the ability to play anything from Bach (you and I both know the answer to his question is **yes**), he stated he may very well come visit us. Will he? God knows. I don’t know, but I am hopeful. For now, what I do have is a few points of reflection:

-   Coffee shops are a great place for a pastor to do work.
-   There are people in our community ready to engage in faith-based conversations.
-   God is moving among the next generation.
-   Join me in prayer for this young man, the next generation, and the community around us as they encounter our Lord, Jesus Christ.

**Youth Group Kick-Off -** _An immersive devotional journey to start off our year…_

The first night of **The Cause** (High School Youth Group) and **Youth Club** (Middle School Bible Study) includes a devotional journey throughout the church. This immersive experience is rooted in our pastoral vision for the ministry year (imitating Christ), and includes the following steps:

-   We start in the Welcome Center, where we talk about the importance of **fellowship** and **loving** **one another**.
-   We then move as a group to our baptistry, sit around it (opened), and talk about what **baptism** is and what it means to die to the self and to live in Christ.
-   The **Communion** Table is our next stop, where we remember what Christ has done for us.
-   Afterward, we head to the Narthex and prayerfully look at the **resurrection** window, and remember how Christ secures our eternal hope in his victory over sin and death.
-   Returning into the sanctuary, we gather at the open Bible towards the front of the sanctuary, and recognize **God’s word** as His revealed message to us, **illuminated** by the Holy Spirit.
-   We trek up to the second floor and into the youth room, where we **pray** to the Lord (using the ACTS acronym).
-   Our last stop is in the second floor chapel, where we **silently reflect** on our devotional journey.

_Pray for us as we begin our ministry year together through this immersive experience._
