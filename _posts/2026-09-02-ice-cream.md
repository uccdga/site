Our second Care Team Volunteer Ice Cream Social was a fun evening filled with ice cream, laughter, and time together!

We were grateful to welcome more than 45 volunteers - over half of our Care Team! Pastor Wade and our intern, Summer, kept the scoops coming, while the topping table was a favorite - especially the vanilla sugar wafers and mini M&M's.

Volunteers wore Mardi Gras beads in the color of the team they serve on, making it easy to see the many areas of Care Ministry represented. It was a relaxed evening to visit with familiar faces, meet volunteers from other teams, and simply enjoy being together.

We plan to make this an annual tradition as part of our ongoing commitment to strengthening relationships among Care Ministry volunteers. If you're not already part of Care Ministry, we'd be glad to help you find a place to connect and serve!
