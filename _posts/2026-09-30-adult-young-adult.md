**Pastoral Reflection -** _My journey toward Imitating Jesus_…

Many of you know by now that my pastoral vision for this ministry year is **imitating Jesus**. This is a natural outflow of last year’s focus, the **Lordship of Jesus**. A spring-time coffee conversation with Jim Kerr about Thomas Kempis (who wrote a book aptly titled “The Imitation of Christ”), along with a late-summer personal devotional study in Ephesians (Eph. 5:1: “_Therefore be imitators of God, as beloved children_”), ultimately proved influential in my discernment process for the next year’s theme. I plan to express this focus through my ministry activities; I hope to become more like Jesus all while inviting others along for the journey.

-   _How do I become more like Jesus?_ Flanked on both sides of Eph. 5:1 are vice lists. I suspect there is meaning behind this format. Ultimately, casting our attention onto the model of Jesus exposes the sin around us. In personal reflection, I notice just how apathetic, impatient, and stubbornly independent I am when exposed by the light of Christ. My journey in imitating Christ is the painful, humbling pivot away from those sins.
-   _How do I invite others along for the journey?_ Those two vice lists meet up with book-ends that echo our baptism (4:22-24 and 5:14 ). Paul also echoes the eucharist just after his instruction to imitate Jesus (5:2). As a pastor, I aim to invite others along for the journey by pointing others to God and their life within Him; the sacraments (or as us Baptists refer to them: the ordinances) do just that. Moving into the ministry year, I strive to view my pastoral assignment, not with a programmatic lens, but a sacramental one. Will you join me?
