Throughout October, November, and December, we make the Stewards Assistance Fund a special focus of our giving. Any loose offering received on the first Sunday of each month is also directed to the fund. These gifts provide confidential support to those who are part of our church family and facing unexpected needs, allowing us to share Christ's love in practical ways. Thank you for your generosity!
