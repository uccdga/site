Our Stewards recently hosted a Fall Fellowship and Fun gathering with our Companions and church friends ages 85 and older.

The day began with worship, a message from Wade, piano duets by Georgia S. and June P., and Communion. Several Companions and Friends shared their thoughts as they completed the sentence, "My heart is satisfied in God because…"

The fun continued in the Dining Hall with lunch, activities, prizes, and a sing-along featuring some favorite oldies, accompanied by piano and guitar.

It was a good day of connection, singing, and laughter.
