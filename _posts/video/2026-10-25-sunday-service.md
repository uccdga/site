The people ask whether they still need to keep the mourning fasts now that the temple is being rebuilt. God's answer moves past the ritual to the heart behind it, and then promises a day when those fasts become feasts. What God wants is not obligation but relationship, worked out in justice and mercy toward the vulnerable.
