The people of Zechariah's day were living in the wreckage of exile, waiting on promises that seemed slow to arrive. This sermon walks through Zechariah 9-10's vision of a coming king, a restored people, and a God who strengthens what was broken, showing how that long wait found its fulfillment in Jesus and still holds hope for whatever we're waiting on today.
