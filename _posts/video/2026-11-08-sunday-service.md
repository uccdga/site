This sermon walks through Zechariah's shepherd allegory, a good shepherd offered and refused, valued at a slave's price, and replaced by one who preys on the flock instead of protecting it. It's an uncomfortable passage that asks what we're willing to pay for the shepherds God gives us, and points ahead to Jesus, the shepherd who never deserts.
