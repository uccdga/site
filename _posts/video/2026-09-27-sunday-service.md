Zechariah begins where Haggai left off, calling a discouraged, still-rebuilding people to turn their hearts back to God, not just their resources. Through two night visions, God reveals that he is already watching over Jerusalem in mercy and preparing to judge the nations who scattered her. The sermon closes by showing that God's return to us was ultimately fulfilled in Christ, who came near before we ever turned back.
