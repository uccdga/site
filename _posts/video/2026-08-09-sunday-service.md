Anxiety is not a modern invention, and Paul's letter to the Philippians offers a timeless path through it. From standing firm in conflict to praying with gratitude to guarding what fills our minds, Philippians 4:1-9 gives the church a practical and gospel-grounded response to anxiety. The promise at the center is not that circumstances will change but that the peace of God will guard our hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.
