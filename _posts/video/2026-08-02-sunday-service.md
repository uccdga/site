Paul writes from prison as a man mid-race, not yet at the finish line, pressing on toward the goal Christ has called him toward. This sermon follows his movement from honest concession to forward-leaning hope, and challenges the congregation to forget what is behind, fix their eyes on their heavenly citizenship, and come to the Lord's Table as fellow runners still running the race.
