This final sermon in the Philippians series looks at Paul's short but weighty closing words, greetings extended to every believer by name and even into Caesar's own household. It's a reminder that the gospel is never contained, no matter the circumstances, and a direct charge for the congregation to consider where God is calling them to live and carry it this week.
