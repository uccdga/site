This chapter meets God's people in the middle of an unfinished, discouraging project and reminds them that presence matters more than appearance. God owns every resource they think they lack, exposes how compromised hearts can quietly spoil even sincere obedience, and promises blessing from a specific day forward. The sermon closes by tracing that hope to Jesus, who touches what's broken and makes it whole instead of being contaminated by it.
