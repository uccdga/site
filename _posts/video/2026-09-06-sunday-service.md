This special service sets the stage for our fall series, The Last Prophets, walking through the biblical story from Abraham to the Babylonian exile to explain why God's people needed to hear from Haggai, Zechariah, and Malachi. It's a story of covenant faithfulness on God's part despite repeated failure on ours, one that still speaks to us today. We close at the Table, asking God to prepare our hearts for the season ahead.
