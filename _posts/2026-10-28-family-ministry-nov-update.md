**Thanksgiving Offering/Mission Project:** During the month of November, our Sunday morning families are invited to take part in our Thanksgiving Offering/Mission Project. This year, we are collecting items for the CCIA and Salvation Army food pantries. Our goal is to collect 50 items for these pantries. **Children and youth are encouraged to bring donations to Sunday school during the month of November.** We will begin receiving items on November 1 and we will count up our donations on November 29. The **most needed items** for **these food pantries are:** cereal, tuna fish, peanut butter, spaghetti sauce, canned soups, canned vegetables, canned fruits, boxed pasta (spaghetti and elbows), mac-n-cheese, canned pork-n-beans, canned meals, toilet paper, toothpaste, deodorant, and shampoo.
